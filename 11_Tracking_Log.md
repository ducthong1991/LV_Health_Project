# Tracking log

Rivaroxaban started about 13 Oct 2026. Fill in one row every Sunday. The doctor will use this to judge the response and decide how long treatment continues.

## How to score

- **Pain:** 0 is none, 10 is the worst imaginable. Record the worst spot.
- **Open wounds:** count them, and measure the largest in millimetres.
- **New spots:** any new red or purple dot, patch or ulcer that week.
- **Missed doses:** how many, and the dates.
