### What it means in plain words

- **Points to vasculitis:** white blood cells (neutrophils) with debris from broken cells ("nuclear dust") around small vessels.
- **Points to LV:** small clots inside tiny veins and fibrin change in a vessel wall.
- **Not seen:** a thickened basement membrane on the special stain (the vessel wall thickening often seen in LV), fungus, granuloma or virus.
- **Medium sized vessels:** some inflammation around them, but no significant damage.
- **Caveat:** the sample came from next to an ulcer. Ulcers cause inflammation that can make LV look like vasculitis.
- **Immunofluorescence:** this pattern is seen in both LV and vasculitis. IgA negative makes IgA vasculitis less likely.
