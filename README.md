# LV Health Project

Built on 21 Nov 2026 from your chats with Claude, your SGH results and bills, and your AIA emails. It is a personal record and general information, not medical, insurance or legal advice.

## How to set up the Project

1. Unzip this folder.
2. In Claude, create a new Project, for example "Leg health and AIA".
3. Open PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md and paste everything below its first line into the Project's instructions.
4. Add files 01 to 12 to the Project's knowledge. Add the files themselves, not the zip.
5. Optional: also add originals/lab_reports/SGH_results_2026-10-26.pdf.
6. Keep the photos and bills for your own records and for AIA. Attach one to a chat when you want Claude to look at it.
7. Leave the archive folder out of the Project. Those are older versions with facts that have since been corrected.

## Keeping it up to date

A new chat in the Project reliably knows only what is in the Project's files and instructions. So after each doctor visit, new result, claim decision or flare:

1. Tell Claude in a Project chat what happened.
2. Ask for updated versions of the affected files. These are usually 01, 02, 09 and 11.
3. Replace the old files in the Project's knowledge.

## Corrections made in this version

These differ from what earlier chats or the older doc said.

- **Rivaroxaban start date.** The SGH invoice shows 14 tablets of rivaroxaban 10 mg dispensed on 13 Oct 2026. Treatment began around 13 Oct, not 26 Oct.
- **First dermatology visit.** It was on 12 Oct 2026. The biopsy and blood tests were on 13 Oct.
- **Football.** Earlier advice said football was fine with shin guards. Contact football is generally advised against while on a blood thinner, so ask the dermatologist first.
- **Sitting breaks.** Short breaks every 30 minutes have better support than 3 to 5 minutes once an hour.
- **Dosing every other day.** Claude advised against this on 10 Nov. One long term study reportedly stepped some patients down to 10 mg every other day. That detail could not be checked again against the paper, so treat it as unconfirmed. Either way, only a doctor should set that schedule.
- **Foot photo date.** The photo shared on 18 Oct has an earlier file number than the 14 Oct photo, so it may have been taken a few days before it was shared.
